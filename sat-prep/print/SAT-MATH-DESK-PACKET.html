2. Math notes (what to have in hand)

Algebra

  • Isolate: expand → combine like terms → divide. If you see 5−2(3x+1), the minus hits both terms inside.
  • Fractions: multiply every term by the LCD.
  • Slope: m=x2​−x1​y2​−y1​​. Parallel = same m. Perpendicular = negative reciprocal, so m1​m2​=−1.
  • y=mx+b: m is the rate (units of y per 1 unit of x); b is the value when x=0.
  • System: 1 solution (intersect), 0 (parallel distinct), infinitely many (same line).
  • Inequality: flip only when multiplying or dividing by a negative.

Advanced Math

  • “Equivalent” = same value for all allowed x. Factor a common binomial, or expand, or graph both.
  • Quadratic ax2+bx+c: zeros from factors; vertex x=−2ab​; vertex form a(x−h)2+k has vertex (h,k).
  • If a>0, minimum is k. If a<0, maximum is k.
  • Exponential: y=A⋅rx. A=y(0). Growth r>1; decay 0<r<1. “+20%” means ×1.20, not +20.
  • Period: A(1+r)kt completes k growth cycles per 1 unit of t. Months per cycle =12/k if t is in years.
  • ∣A∣=k: if k<0, no solution; if k=0, one; if k>0, two cases.
  • Rational: multiply by LCD; throw away any x that makes a denominator 0.
  • Radical: isolate, square, check in the original (extraneous roots are a real SAT skill).

PSDA

  • Ratio a:b with first part A: ba​=BA​.
  • Combined rate: add the rates, then time =combined ratework​.
  • Percent of: part =p× whole. Percent change =oldnew−old​.
  • Successive percents multiply: up 10% then up 10% is ×1.1×1.1=1.21, not +20%.
  • “Increase x by p%” means x(1+100p​). Increase by 400% is ×5, not ×4.
  • “30% of the 40% that are red” is 0.3×0.4=0.12, not 40−30.
  • Mean follows outliers; median often does not. Mean of n values ⇒ sum =n⋅mean.
  • Two-way table: P(A) uses the grand total; P(A∣B) first restrict to the B row or column.
  • Margin of error: 52% ± 4% means the true value is likely between 48% and 56%, not “exactly 52%.”

Geometry and trigonometry

  • Linear scale k ⇒ area ×k2, volume ×k3.
  • Similar figures: sides and perimeter ×k; angles stay the same.
  • Triangle angles sum to 180∘.
  • Pythagorean: a2+b2=c2. Families: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 6-8-10, 9-12-15.
  • 45-45-90: legs s, hypotenuse s2​.
  • 30-60-90: short s (opp. 30°), long s3​, hypotenuse 2s.
  • sinθ=hypopp​, cosθ=hypadj​, tanθ=adjopp​. Also cosθ=sin(90∘−θ).
  • Circle: C=2πr=πd, A=πr2, arc =360θ​⋅2πr, sector =360θ​⋅πr2.
  • (x−h)2+(y−k)2=r2: center (h,k), radius r.
  • Tangent at P: the radius to P is perpendicular to the tangent. Find mradius​, then mtangent​=−mradius​1​, then point-slope at P (not at the center).
