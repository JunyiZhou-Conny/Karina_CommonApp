1. Notes for this set only

Successive percent

  • Each change multiplies. Do not add the percents.
  • Up p% means ×(1+100p​). Down p% means ×(1−100p​).
  • Up 10% then up 10% is ×1.1×1.1=1.21, a 21% increase, not +20%.
  • Up 10% then down 10% is ×1.1×0.9=0.99. You do not return to the start.

“Increase by p%”

  • Increase x by p% means x(1+100p​).
  • Increase by 100% = double (×2). Increase by 400% = ×5.
  • “400% of x” is a different sentence (×4).

Exponential period

  • A(1+r)kt completes k growth cycles in one unit of t.
  • If t is in years, months per cycle =12/k.
  • Example: exponent 2t ⇒ 2 cycles per year ⇒ every 6 months.

Tangent to a circle

  • The radius to the contact point P is perpendicular to the tangent at P.
  • Perpendicular slopes: m1​m2​=−1 (negative reciprocal). Flip the fraction and change the sign.
  • Vertical radius ⇒ horizontal tangent (slope 0).
  • Write the tangent with point-slope at P, not at the center. Then test points in that line.
