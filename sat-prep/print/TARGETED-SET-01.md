## 1. Notes for this set only

### Successive percent

- Each change **multiplies**. Do not add the percents.
- Up \(p\%\) means \(\times\bigl(1+\frac{p}{100}\bigr)\). Down \(p\%\) means \(\times\bigl(1-\frac{p}{100}\bigr)\).
- Up 10% then up 10% is \(\times 1.1\times 1.1=1.21\), a **21%** increase, not \(+20\%\).
- Up 10% then down 10% is \(\times 1.1\times 0.9=0.99\). You do **not** return to the start.

### “Increase by \(p\%\)”

- Increase \(x\) by \(p\%\) means \(x\bigl(1+\frac{p}{100}\bigr)\).
- Increase by 100% = double (\(\times 2\)). Increase by 400% = \(\times 5\).
- “400% **of** \(x\)” is a different sentence (\(\times 4\)).

### Exponential period

- \(A(1+r)^{kt}\) completes \(k\) growth cycles in one unit of \(t\).
- If \(t\) is in years, months per cycle \(=12/k\).
- Example: exponent \(2t\) \(\Rightarrow\) 2 cycles per year \(\Rightarrow\) every 6 months.

### Tangent to a circle

- The radius to the contact point \(P\) is **perpendicular** to the tangent at \(P\).
- Perpendicular slopes: \(m_1 m_2=-1\) (negative reciprocal). Flip the fraction and change the sign.
- Vertical radius \(\Rightarrow\) horizontal tangent (slope \(0\)).
- Write the tangent with point-slope **at \(P\)**, not at the center. Then test points in that line.
