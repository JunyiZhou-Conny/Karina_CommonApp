# Weekend 1 — Sunday (4 hours)

**Theme:** Mixed diagnostic + nitpick clinic across **all four** domains  
**Skills:** A · B · C (PSDA) · D  
**Mode:** Timed reps, then student-explains-the-miss. Still not a from-scratch lecture.

---

## Block 0 — Saturday miss review (0:00–0:20)

Re-work every Saturday miss **cold** (no notes). If still wrong → fix the micro-gap immediately.

### Fast-fail checklist (whiteboard)

1. Did I distribute the negative?  
2. Did I flip the inequality?  
3. Did I check the solution in the **original** equation?  
4. For systems: did I solve for **both** variables?  
5. Percent of *what*? Conditional probability: which row?  
6. Scale factor: length vs area vs volume?  
7. SPR: sign, fraction, decimal — what Bluebook will accept?

## Block 1 — Timed mixed diagnostic (0:20–0:45) · all four domains

**12 questions, 18 minutes.** Calculator/Desmos allowed. Student works silently. You only watch pacing.

**D1.** (A1) \(7-3(2x-1)=2x+8\)

**D2.** (A4) Solution of \(\begin{cases}x+y=10\\ 2x-y=5\end{cases}\)

**D3.** (A5) \(-\dfrac12 x+3\le 7\). Solution set?

**D4.** (B1) Factor \(x^2-5x-14\). Enter the **smaller** constant term of the two binomials (SPR).

**D5.** (B2) \(x\)-coordinate of the vertex of \(y=x^2-8x+10\)

**D6.** (B4) Population starts at 200 and grows 50% each decade. Population after 2 decades? (SPR)

**D7.** (C1) A car goes 180 miles in 3 hours. At that rate, miles in 2.5 hours? (SPR)

**D8.** (C2) A \$60 jacket is discounted 15%, then tax of 8% is added to the sale price. Final price?

**D9.** (C5) From Saturday’s club table (20 juniors, 30 seniors; 30 Yes, 20 No). \(P(\text{Yes})\) for a random member?

**D10.** (D3) Legs 5 and 12. Hypotenuse?

**D11.** (D2) A triangle has angles \(40^\circ\) and \(65^\circ\). Third angle?

**D12.** (D4) Circumference of a circle with diameter 10, in terms of \(\pi\).

## Block 2 — Nitpick clinic on the diagnostic (0:45–1:40)

Go item by item **only where she missed or hesitated**. Student explains first.

### Instructor prompts (use these; don’t redo a lecture)

- “Point to the first decision you made.”  
- “Which domain is this, and why does that change the tool?”  
- “If SAT swapped one word, where would you break?”  
- “Desmos or hand? Why?”

### Likely 680-band leaks (check even if she got them right)

| Item | Typical leak |
|---|---|
| D1 | Minus times a grouped term |
| D3 | Forgot to flip |
| D6 | Used \(+50\) instead of \(\times 1.5\) |
| D8 | Taxed the original \$60 instead of the sale price |
| D9 | Used a row (conditional) instead of the whole table |
| D10 | \(5+12=17\) instead of Pythagorean |

If the diagnostic is mostly clean, spend leftover time on **harder oral variants** of D6, D8, D9.

## Block 3 — Four-domain pressure stations (1:50–3:10)

Three items per domain. Student talks the setup **before** calculating. You nitpick the setup.

### Station A — Algebra

**U1.** Phone plan \$35/month + \$0.10 per text. Total \$47. How many texts?  
**U2.** Which line is perpendicular to \(y=3x-1\) and passes through \((0,4)\)?  
 A) \(y=3x+4\) B) \(y=-\tfrac13 x+4\) C) \(y=\tfrac13 x+4\) D) \(y=-3x+4\)  
**U3.** Adult tickets \$12, child \$7. 15 tickets cost \$145. How many adult tickets? (SPR)

### Station B — Advanced Math

**U4.** Expand \((x-4)^2\).  
**U5.** A ball’s height \(h(t)=-16t^2+32t+5\) (feet, seconds). When does it hit the ground? (exact form OK)  
**U6.** Table \(x=0,1,2,3\) and \(y=12,18,27,40.5\). Linear or exponential? Write a model.

### Station C — PSDA

**U7.** 18 is 45% of what number?  
**U8.** Scores: 70, 74, 74, 76, 92. Mean vs median. Which should you quote if you want the “typical” score without the 92?  
**U9.** A study asks only students in the honors hallway whether they like the new schedule, then claims “the school supports it.” What is the design problem? (C7)

### Station D — Geometry & Trig

**U10.** Similar triangles. Corresponding sides 4 and 10. Smaller perimeter 18. Larger perimeter?  
**U11.** 30-60-90 triangle, short leg 6. Long leg and hypotenuse?  
**U12.** Right triangle, adjacent 8, hypotenuse 10. \(\cos\theta=\) ?

## Block 4 — SPR + Desmos + Module 1 habits (3:10–3:50)

### SPR drill (8 minutes, answers only)

**P1.** \(0.2(x+15)=0.5x-3\)

**P3.** Bacteria double every 3 hours from 100. How many after 9 hours?

2. Graph \(y=x^2\) and \(y=x+2\); confirm Saturday S11.  
3. Graph \(y=|2x-3|\) and \(y=7\); confirm S10.

3. What do you do at 2:30 stuck?  
4. Name one PSDA wording trap and one Geometry scale-factor trap from today.
