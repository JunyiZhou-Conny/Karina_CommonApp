## Block 1 — Linear functions in context (0:20–1:10) · Skill A3

### Objective
Translate science/social “story” → linear model \(y=mx+b\) or \(ax+by=c\); answer the question asked (value, rate, intercept, intersection).

### Mini-lesson pattern
1. Define variables with units.  
2. Write equation(s).  
3. Solve.  
4. Re-read the question — did they want \(x\), \(y\), a difference, or “after how many…”?

### Worked example
A tank starts with 80 L and drains at 2.5 L/min.  
\(V=80-2.5t\). When is \(V=30\)?  
\(80-2.5t=30\) → \(t=20\) minutes.

### Practice

**U1.** Phone plan: \$35/month + \$0.10 per text. Total \$47. How many texts?  
**U2.** Two printers: A prints 40 pages/min, B 55. They start together and need 600 pages. Minutes until done if both run? *(Careful: combined rate.)*  
**U3.** Line of best fit for study hours \(x\) and score \(y\): \(y=8x+42\). Interpret slope. Predict score for 6 hours.  
**U4.** (SPR) A car is 180 miles from home, driving toward home at 45 mph. Miles from home after 2.5 hours?

## Block 2 — Equivalent expressions / factoring bridge (1:10–2:00) · Skill B1

### Objective
Rewrite expressions: distribute, factor GCF, factor trinomials, difference of squares. Recognize equivalent MC choices.

### Mini-lesson
SAT loves: “Which expression is equivalent to …?”  
Strategy: expand all choices **or** factor the stem — pick the cleaner path. Desmos: compare \(y=\) expressions; identical graphs ⇒ equivalent.

### Worked examples
1. \((2x-3)(x+5)=2x^2+7x-15\)  
2. \(x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)\)  
3. \(6x^2+15x=3x(2x+5)\)

### Practice

**U5.** Which is equivalent to \(4x(x-3)-2(x-3)\)?  
 A) \(2(x-3)(2x-1)\) B) \(2(x-3)(2x+1)\) C) \((x-3)(4x-2)\) D) Both A and C  
**U6.** Factor completely: \(3x^2-12x-36\).  
**U7.** Expand: \((x-4)^2\).  
**U8.** Simplify: \(\dfrac{6x^2-9x}{3x}\) for \(x\neq 0\).

## Block 3 — Quadratics (2:10–3:10) · Skills B2 / B4

### Objective
Connect forms: standard \(ax^2+bx+c\), factored \(a(x-r)(x-s)\), vertex \(a(x-h)^2+k\). Find zeros, vertex, axis of symmetry. Use Desmos to verify.

### Mini-lesson
- Zeros = \(x\)-intercepts = roots  
- Axis: \(x=-\dfrac{b}{2a}\)  
- Vertex \(y\)-value: plug axis into function  
- If \(a>0\), opens up (min); \(a<0\), opens down (max)

### Worked example
\(f(x)=x^2-6x+8=(x-2)(x-4)\).  
Zeros: \(2,4\). Axis: \(x=3\). Vertex: \(f(3)=-1\) → \((3,-1)\).

Desmos: graph; confirm.

### Practice

**U9.** Zeros of \(x^2+x-12=0\).  
**U10.** Vertex of \(y=-2(x+1)^2+5\). Max or min?  
**U11.** A ball’s height \(h(t)=-16t^2+32t+5\) (feet, seconds). When does it hit the ground? *(Quadratic formula / Desmos.)*  
**U12.** Write quadratic with zeros \(-3\) and \(5\) and \(a=1\) in standard form.  
**U13.** (SPR) For \(y=x^2-4x-1\), the minimum value of \(y\) is?

## Block 4 — Exponential vs linear (3:10–3:50) · Skill B4

### Objective
Distinguish \(y=mx+b\) (constant difference) vs \(y=a\cdot r^x\) (constant ratio). Interpret growth/decay rate.

### Mini-lesson
- Linear: +same amount each step  
- Exponential: ×same factor each step  
- Growth: \(r>1\); decay: \(0<r<1\)  
- “Increases by 20% per year” → \(r=1.20\)

### Worked example
Population \(P=500(1.06)^t\).  
Initial: 500. Annual growth: 6%. After 3 years: \(500(1.06)^3\approx 595.5\).

### Practice

**U14.** Which models exponential decay?  
 A) \(y=20-3x\) B) \(y=20(0.85)^x\) C) \(y=20(1.15)^x\) D) \(y=3x+20\)  
**U15.** Table: \(x=0,1,2,3\) and \(y=12,18,27,40.5\). Linear or exponential? Write a model.  
**U16.** (SPR) Bacteria double every 3 hours starting at 100. How many after 9 hours?  
**U17.** Rewrite \(y=5\cdot 2^{x}\) as \(y=5\cdot e^{kx}\) is optional (usually not needed); instead: find \(y\) when \(x=4\).
