# Weekend 1 — Optional Algebra drill bank

Use **only if** Saturday Block 1 showed sloppy linear fluency and you want extra reps.  
These are the original Weekend 1 Algebra items (instructor-created). Answers are in [`answer-key-01.md`](answer-key-01.md) § Optional Algebra drill.

**A1**

1. \(5-2(3x+1)=x-17\)  
2. \(\dfrac{x}{4}+\dfrac{x}{6}=5\)  
3. (SPR) \(0.2(x+15)=0.5x-3\)  
4. A number decreased by 18 is four times the number. Find the number.  
5. \(\dfrac{3(2x-1)}{5}=x+1\)

**A2–A3**

6. Write \(3x-2y=12\) in slope-intercept form; state slope and \(y\)-intercept.  
7. Line with slope \(-\tfrac{1}{2}\) through \((-4,7)\). Equation in point-slope then slope-intercept.  
8. Cost \(C=12n+45\) for \(n\) shirts. Interpret slope and intercept. What is \(C\) when \(n=8\)?  
9. Which line is perpendicular to \(y=3x-1\) and passes through \((0,4)\)?  
 A) \(y=3x+4\) B) \(y=-\tfrac13 x+4\) C) \(y=\tfrac13 x+4\) D) \(y=-3x+4\)

**A4**

10. \(\begin{cases}y=2x-3\\ 3x+2y=26\end{cases}\)  
11. \(\begin{cases}4x+3y=10\\ 2x-y=1\end{cases}\)  
12. How many solutions? \(\begin{cases}2x-4y=6\\ x-2y=5\end{cases}\)  
13. (SPR) Adult tickets \$12, child \$7. 15 tickets cost \$145. How many adult tickets?

**A5**

14. Solve: \(2(x-4)<5x+1\).  
15. Which point satisfies \(y>2x-1\) and \(x+y\le 4\)?  
 A) \((0,5)\) B) \((1,2)\) C) \((3,0)\) D) \((2,3)\)  
16. For which \(k\) is \(x=2\) a solution of \(3x+k>10\)?
