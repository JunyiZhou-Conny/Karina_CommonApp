## Block 1 — Right-triangle trig (0:15–1:10) · D3

### Oral
1. SOH-CAH-TOA, out loud.  
2. \(\sin\theta=\cos(90^\circ-\theta)\). Why does SAT like that?  
3. You know \(\sin\theta=3/5\). A possible opposite and hypotenuse? Need the third side?

### Items

**U1.** Opposite 3, hypotenuse 5. \(\sin\theta=\) ?

**U2.** Adjacent 8, hypotenuse 10. \(\cos\theta=\) ?

**U3.** Opposite 3, adjacent 4. \(\tan\theta=\) ? Hypotenuse?

**U4.** \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{5}{13}\). \(\cos\theta=\) ? (right triangle, acute \(\theta\))

**U5.** (SPR) In a right triangle, \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{3}{4}\). Enter \(\sin\theta\) as a decimal.

**U6.** Which is equal to \(\cos 20^\circ\)?  
 A) \(\sin 20^\circ\) B) \(\sin 70^\circ\) C) \(\tan 20^\circ\) D) \(\cos 70^\circ\)

### Nitpick
- Label opposite/adjacent **relative to the angle named**  
- Pythagorean to finish the third side before finding the other ratio

## Block 2 — Circles (1:10–2:00) · D4

### Oral
1. \(C=2\pi r=\pi d\). \(A=\pi r^2\).  
2. Arc length \(=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\cdot 2\pi r\).  
3. Sector area \(=\dfrac{\theta}{360}\cdot \pi r^2\).  
4. Circle equation \((x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2\). Center and radius?

### Items

**U7.** Diameter 10. Circumference in terms of \(\pi\)? (SPR: coefficient of \(\pi\))

**U8.** Radius 4. Area in terms of \(\pi\)?

**U9.** Radius 6, central angle 60°. Arc length?

**U10.** Same circle, 60° sector area in terms of \(\pi\)? (SPR: coefficient of \(\pi\))

**U11.** \((x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=16\). Center and radius?

**U12.** A central angle of 90° on a circle of radius 8. Arc length in terms of \(\pi\)?

### Nitpick
- Diameter vs radius (U7 is the classic rush miss)  
- 60° is \(\tfrac16\) of the circle, 90° is \(\tfrac14\)

## Block 4 — Adaptive / pacing clinic (3:10–3:50)

No new content. Oral + two “decision” reps.

### Decision rules (write on the board)

1. **Module 1 is a routing test.** Two careless Algebra misses can cap the 780.  
2. First pass: bank every A and easy C/D.  
3. Desmos when: systems, “which graph,” intersections, “equivalent” check.  
4. Hand algebra when: one-step isolate, percent, table probability.  
5. 2:30 stuck → flag, guess if needed, move. Come back if time.  
6. SPR: never leave blank.  
7. Last 60 seconds: any blank SPR + any unanswered MC.

### Two pacing reps (talk, don’t fully solve unless useful)

**R1.** A long exponential word problem appears as question 6 of 22. Do you grind it now?  
**R2.** Question 20 is a circle sector and you have 4 minutes + two unanswered Algebra SPRs. Order of operations?
