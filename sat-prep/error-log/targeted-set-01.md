# Targeted set 01 — only current gaps

**Karina.** Do this set. Do not go back through the full desk packet.

Calculator / Desmos is fine. SPR = type a number.  
These are instructor-written SAT-style items. They are **not** official College Board questions.

Skills in this set only:

- successive percent (multiply, don’t add)
- “increase by \(p\%\)” wording
- exponential growth period
- tangent to a circle \(\perp\) radius; product of perpendicular slopes is \(-1\)

---

**1.** (SPR) A club has 400 members. Membership increases 20% in year 1 and increases 20% again in year 2. How many members are there after 2 years?

**2.** A \$50 price increases by 20%, then the new price decreases by 20%. What is the final price?  
A) \$50  
B) \$48  
C) \$52  
D) \$40

**3.** The result of increasing the quantity \(x\) by 300% is 80. What is the value of \(x\)?  
A) \(\dfrac{80}{3}\)  
B) 20  
C) 240  
D) 320

**4.** (SPR) A population starts at 80 and grows by 25% each year. What is the population after 2 years?

**5.** The function \(Q\) is defined by \(Q(t)=500(1.06)^{2t}\), where \(t\) is the number of years after 2020. According to the model, \(Q\) increases by 6% every \(n\) months. What is the value of \(n\)?  
A) 2  
B) 6  
C) 12  
D) 24

**6.** The function \(P\) is defined by \(P(t)=300(1.08)^{3t}\), where \(t\) is years after 2010. The model increases the population by 8% every \(n\) months. What is \(n\)?  
A) 3  
B) 4  
C) 8  
D) 36

**7.** (SPR) A circle has center \((0,0)\). A line is tangent to the circle at \((3,4)\). What is the slope of the tangent line?

**8.** A circle in the \(xy\)-plane has center \((2,3)\). Line \(\ell\) is tangent to the circle at \((6,6)\). Which point also lies on \(\ell\)?  
A) \((2,3)\)  
B) \((3,10)\)  
C) \((8,9)\)  
D) \((5,3)\)

**9.** (SPR) A circle has center \((-2,1)\). A tangent line touches the circle at \((1,5)\). The equation of the tangent line can be written as \(y=mx+b\). What is the value of \(b\)?

**10.** A radius of a circle runs from \((1,2)\) to the point of tangency \((5,4)\). What is the slope of the tangent line at \((5,4)\)?  
A) \(\dfrac{1}{2}\)  
B) \(2\)  
C) \(-2\)  
D) \(-\dfrac{1}{2}\)

**11.** A circle has center \((4,1)\). A line is tangent to the circle at \((4,7)\). What is the slope of the tangent line?  
A) \(0\)  
B) \(1\)  
C) undefined  
D) \(6\)

**12.** A jacket costs \$40. The store marks it up 10%, then takes 10% off the **new** price. How much less than \$40 is the final price?  
A) \$0  
B) \$0.40  
C) \$4  
D) \$8
