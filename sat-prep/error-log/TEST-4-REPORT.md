## What to emphasize

**Exponential period, both directions (K3).** Test 5 hid the months and gave the percent. This item hid the percent and gave 18 months. Same clock: one cycle when the exponent equals 1. The coefficient of \(t\) and the period in years are reciprocals. Future original: hide the coefficient, give \(r\%\) per 18 months, ask for the exponent.

**Shift vs spread (K25).** Adding 56 to every value moves median (and mean). Range does not change. The stem asked median and **range**, not mean.

**Recover, then shift (K26).** Not a parabola. Two jobs: read \(a\) and \(b\) off the graph, then replace \(x\) by \(x+4\). Stopping at recovered \(f\) is choice B on this form.

**\(f(1)\) plus sign of \(a\) (K27).** \(a+b+c\) is the \(y\)-value at \(x=1\). Vertex below the axis and two \(x\)-intercepts force \(a>0\), which kills every choice \(\le\) the vertex \(y\).

Vieta **product** \(c/a\) is landing (#19). Set 03 leftover **K23** is still the **sign of the sum** \(-\frac{b}{a}\).
