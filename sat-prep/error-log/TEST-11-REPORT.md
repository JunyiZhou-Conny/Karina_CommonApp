## What to emphasize (your notes, filed)

**Trig (M1 #19 + M2 #26).** Same family. M2 #26 is the typical trap: SAT hands out 429 and 572 and asks for a tangent or cosine. Students reach for Pythagorean and a giant hypotenuse. The item wants the **ratio** (similar right triangles / altitude to the hypotenuse). The numbers cancel.

**Estimate (M2 #19).** Mass from a graph that is not a clean integer. She has to live with a little ambiguity and pick closest. This type shows up often.

**Systems (M1 #25).** Infinite solutions vs one vs none vs at least one. Scale-check the second equation.

**Units (M2 #27).** Linear factor vs area factor. Tricky.

**Angle combinations (M1 #24).** Four choices are four possible pairings. Read closely; list every legal sum; kill the one that cannot happen.
