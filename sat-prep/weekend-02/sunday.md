## Block 1 — Exponential equations & models (0:20–1:15) · B2 / B4

### Oral
1. “Grows by 6% per year” → what multiplier?  
2. Same-base move: \(2^{x+1}=16\) — rewrite 16 first.  
3. Linear vs exponential from a table: differences vs ratios.

### Items

**U1.** Solve \(2^{x+1}=16\).

**U2.** Solve \(5\cdot 3^x=45\).

**U3.** \(P=100\left(\dfrac12\right)^t\). For which \(t\) is \(P=25\)?

**U4.** Which models exponential growth?  
 A) \(y=9-0.2x\) B) \(y=9(0.8)^x\) C) \(y=9(1.2)^x\) D) \(y=9/x\)

**U5.** (SPR) Bacteria start at 80 and triple every 4 hours. How many after 12 hours?

**U6.** A car worth \$20{,}000 depreciates 15% per year. Write a model \(V(t)\). Find \(V(2)\).

### Nitpick
- \(+6\%\) is \(\times 1.06\), not \(+6\)  
- Half-life / doubling: count **how many periods**, not clock hours blindly

## Block 2 — Nonlinear systems (1:15–2:05) · B3

### Oral
1. Line + parabola: 0, 1, or 2 intersections. How do you see it in Desmos?  
2. Substitution vs graphing: when each?  
3. What does a repeated root mean geometrically?

### Items

**U7.** Solve \(\begin{cases}y=x^2\\ y=2x+3\end{cases}\)

**U8.** How many solutions: \(\begin{cases}y=x^2+1\\ y=x\end{cases}\)?  
 A) 0 B) 1 C) 2 D) 3

**U9.** Solve \(\begin{cases}x+y=5\\ y=x^2-1\end{cases}\)

**U10.** (SPR) The **larger** \(x\)-value in the intersection of \(y=x^2-4\) and \(y=x\).

**U11.** A circle \(x^2+y^2=25\) and line \(y=3\). How many intersection points? Find them.

### Nitpick
- After substituting, you still have to **pair** \(y\) with each \(x\)  
- U8: \(x^2-x+1=0\) discriminant \(1-4<0\) → none — a graph would show the line missing the parabola

## Block 3 — Reading nonlinear functions (2:15–3:05) · B4

### Oral
1. \(f(x)=a(x-h)^2+k\): what do \(a,h,k\) do?  
2. \(g(x)=A\cdot r^x\): what is \(g(0)\)? What is \(r\)?  
3. SAT asks “which statement is true about the function” — they want a **parameter meaning**, not a new calculation.

### Items

**U12.** \(f(x)=2(x-1)^2+5\). Vertex? Min or max? \(f(3)\)?

**U13.** \(g(x)=3\cdot 2^x\). Find \(g(0)\) and \(g(4)\). Interpret 3 and 2.

**U14.** A ball \(h(t)=-16t^2+48t+4\). What is the **maximum** height? (Desmos or vertex.)

**U15.** Which function has a \(y\)-intercept of 7 and decreases by a constant factor?  
 A) \(y=7-2x\) B) \(y=7(0.6)^x\) C) \(y=7+0.6x\) D) \(y=0.6x+7\)

**U16.** (SPR) For \(y=-(x+2)^2+9\), the maximum value of \(y\).
