# SAT Math Curriculum Roadmap

**Student:** Karina · **Start Math:** ~680 · **Target:** ~780  
**Cadence:** Saturday 4h + Sunday 4h  
**Model:** She already knows the school-math. We **survey, nitpick, then drill** — not teach from zero.

**PSDA** = **Problem-Solving and Data Analysis** (ratios, percents, data, probability, statistical claims).
